Mallow Munch: Privacy Policy

Developer: VSZANZ · Last updated 7 November 2026

Mallow Munch is built to respect your privacy. In short, we do not collect your personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no account, no analytics, no advertising network and no tracking. The app does not use the internet and does not send any data off your device.

What is saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game stores a few things on your phone only: your nickname, coins, best run, best stage, the hippos and diamonds you own, your unlocked artwork, puzzle progress and settings. This never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or pressing Reset in Settings removes it.

Nickname

Your nickname is shown only to you on the home screen. Please do not use your real name or other personal details.

Ads

The "ad" screen in the game is a simple on-screen timer. It does not load any third-party ad network and shares no information.

Contact developer

The contact screen only prepares a message for you. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it yourself from your own email app.

Purchases and children

There are no purchases with real money, no chat and no links to other sites. The game is designed for players aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
