Mallow Munch: Privacy Policy

Developer: VSZANZ · Last updated 8 November 2026

Mallow Munch is built to respect your privacy. We, the developer, do not collect your personal information. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show those ads, as explained below.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. There is no sign-up, no account and no analytics. The game itself works offline; the app only uses the internet to load ads.

What is saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game stores a few things on your phone only: your nickname, coins, best run, best stage, the hippos and diamonds you own, your unlocked artwork, puzzle progress and settings. This never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or pressing Reset in Settings removes it.

Nickname

Your nickname is shown only to you on the home screen. Please do not use your real name or other personal details.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional ad you can watch to continue a game, and a short full-screen ad every few stages. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

• your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
• approximate location (from your IP address)
• information about ad views and interactions, and app performance and crash data

In the UK, the European Economic Area, Switzerland and some other regions, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Contact developer

The contact screen only prepares a message for you. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it yourself from your own email app.

Purchases and children

There are no purchases with real money and no chat. The game is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
